## Salon SaaS – Booking & CRM Platform

### What you get

- Online booking linked to your staff schedules.
- Customer profiles and visit history.
- Service catalog with prices and durations.
- Automated confirmations and reminders (via n8n).
- Reports for daily revenue, staff performance, and top services.

### How onboarding works

1. We create your salon in the system and your admin login.
2. You add staff, working hours, and services.
3. We connect your domain and reminder workflows (SMS/email).

### Suggested pricing

- Starter: up to 3 staff – $49/month.
- Growing: up to 10 staff – $99/month.
- Pro: unlimited staff and advanced automation – $149/month.
